## R33i (WORK v2: the counter must never jump when the strip is scrolled back and forth) (use Sonnet 5.5, quick mode; branch `work-v2`: commit there, never merge to main)

Anh's report: "the counter number is jumping if I scroll back and forth too much". Evidence: `docs/reference/counter-jump-recording.mp4` (880x362, 30 fps, ~5 s, a screen recording of /work; the counter is the big numeral) and a contact sheet of 36 frames at 20 fps, `docs/reference/counter-jump-sheet.png` (read left to right, top to bottom). What the sheet shows (frames from 0.3 s): the units drum is turning 1 -> 2 and gets almost to "02" (frame 7: the 2 is nearly landed, only a sliver of the 1 above), and in the NEXT frame (50 ms later) it shows a settled "01": the drum went back about 0.8 of a number in one frame, a snap, not a roll. The same kind of snap shows later (a 1 -> 2 roll that disappears, then "01" again, then "02" rolls in for real).

### Likely causes (check, do not assume)
`setCounter` (v2-ui.js) is a spring on D toward `target` = `s.kNum` ("nearest project while moving, the heading one after the stop"). Two things can make a visible snap when the strip is moved back and forth: (a) the target flips 1 <-> 2 every time the strip passes the half-way point between two projects, so with small back-and-forth scrolls the target toggles several times a second and the spring (settle 0.35 s, speed cap `counterMaxSteps` 12 numbers/s) pulls the drum back as fast as the cap allows: 0.8 of a number in 50-70 ms reads as a jump; (b) at the scroll stop the target changes from "nearest" to "heading" in one frame, which can be one number away from where the drum is heading (the cap then becomes max(12, |delta| / 0.5) numbers/s, even faster).

### What it must do
1. Log first: a script (tools/scratch/r33i-counter.mjs, Playwright, as the other r33 scripts) that scripts back-and-forth wheel input (small and large amplitude, fast and slow, in both modes, 20+ reversals, also reversals started in the middle of a roll and right at the half-way point), and records per frame: the target, D, the speed DV, and the displayed value of both digits. Report the worst per-frame change of D (in numbers) before the fix and the number of frames where |dD| > 0.35 (a roll faster than about 0.35 numbers per 16.7 ms is a jump).
2. Fix so the drum is ALWAYS a smooth, continuous roll: D and its speed continuous (a reversal slows down to zero and rolls back, never a step); no frame where the drum moves more than a limit per frame that you choose and report (suggest: at most 0.25 numbers per 16.7 ms = 15 numbers/s, below the current cap's effect on reversals); the same for the scroll-stop re-target.
3. Stop the target flipping on small wiggles: add hysteresis to "nearest project while moving": the target only changes to the next project once the strip is past that project's half-way point by `counterHysteresis` (new, in pitches, start 0.15; ?tune slider 0-0.4 in WORK_COUNTER), and goes back only when it is that far back on the other side. A real, deliberate scroll still counts every number it passes (no skipped numbers), the wrap 05 <-> 01 stays ONE direct slide, the odometer look, the tens digit and the "/N" are unchanged.
4. The reversal must also be right at the wrap (05 -> 01 and back) and with `?n=12`.
5.
