1. No enlarge after the toggle. Anh: after a toggle the project image should be at its normal stage (scale 1), not enlarging, so the focus build does not drag the settle movement of the images and cards. Cause: the toggle ends with the nearest project centred and at rest, so the focus effect (enlarge, card push, neighbour move, project name) starts building right as the settle plays. Fix: after a toggle the focus effect (and the ambient drift) stays OFF (fP held at 0, name hidden, no push, no drift) from the toggle start until the NEXT input (wheel, key, drag, click on the strip) after the morph and its settle are over; the next rest after that input builds the focus as usual. The first project at page load still starts focused as now. Tunable: WORK.focusAfterMorph ('input' = until the next input, default; or a number of seconds after the settle ends, so Anh can try a delay; 0 = as today) with a description in tune-docs.js; put it in the Active group. Test: toggle in both directions at rest and mid-scroll: fP stays 0 and the image scale stays 1.0 for the whole morph and settle and until the next input (log every frame), the settle curve is unchanged (speed never rises again, ends exactly on place), after a wheel / drag + rest the focus builds normally, the counter order, snap and seam still hold; with a number of seconds, the focus starts building that long after the settle ends.
2. Drag release coasts with friction instead of the planned move + overshoot. Now: on release the strip travels the pointer speed x dragInertia (0.5 s) along an easeOutCubic over 1.5 s and ends with the dragOvershoot bump (scaled by the release force): it brakes toward a computed point, then bumps past it and returns, which reads as a brake, not as momentum running out; the cards only follow through their depth weight. New: after release the strip keeps its speed and loses it by friction (exponential decay, WORK.dragFriction, start 0.6 s): distance = release speed x friction time, so a hard fling carries far and a slow one hardly coasts; it ends naturally when the speed falls under about 5 px/s (no overshoot, no bump; dragOvershoot is removed for the release, the snap / morph / focus overshoots stay). Cap the coast distance (WORK.dragMaxCoast, start 1.5 pitches) so a very hard fling cannot throw the strip across all five projects. Cards keep drifting too: each card coasts with its own friction, a little lighter for nearer cards (WORK.dragCardFrictionVar, start +-0.25 by depth weight), so the cards glide slightly further than the strip and settle after it, then everything slows down together (layered parallax). Continuity: the strip's speed at the moment of release equals its last drag speed (no step) and then only falls (never rises again, the same rule as the toggle settle). Hand-over to the soft snap: it takes over after the usual rest; add the option WORK.dragSnapBlend (0 = off, default; otherwise the coasting speed in px/s under which the snap may start, suggested 80) so the snap can start as the coast slows, making the strip feel magnetised to the nearest project; Anh will compare. 3. Cards move at their own speed while scrolling (speed lead). Anh: when scrolling, the cards should move on their own speed, faster than the strip; now they move rigidly along with it. Option A chosen (NOT permanent parallax: on the endless looped strip that would make the cards drift away from their project, break the seam and the layout rules). While the strip is moving each card gets extra travel ALONG the strip proportional to the scroll speed; it runs ahead of the images; when the strip slows or stops each card eases back through its own spring to its place beside its project. Per card (seeded from its hash + WORK.seed + project, a duplicate has its own set): the lead factor is drawn from WORK.leadMin 0.1 to WORK.leadMax 0.5 (i.e. 1.1x to 1.5x the strip's speed at the peak of a flick) times the depth weight of the mouse drift (nearer cards run further), its own spring (WORK.leadStiffness / leadDamping with a seeded variation WORK.leadVar 0.2 so the cards do not return in lockstep). The lead offset is bounded: |offset| <= WORK.leadMaxPx (start 160 px at 1920, scaled with the capped scale). The speed that drives it is the strip's own motion: wheel / Lenis glide, drag, and the R22i drag coast (cards glide ahead and settle after the strip); NOT the soft snap drift, the toggle morph, the ambient drift or the focus push (those keep their own motion; zero lead while they run). It is a visual offset added like the mouse drift (S, snap, counter, focus, seam never see it); it must not pop when it starts or when another motion takes over (frame by frame).
