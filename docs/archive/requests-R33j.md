## R33j (WORK v2: the image warp must settle monotonically to flat, no flip-flop on scroll up) (use Sonnet 5.5, quick mode; branch `work-v2`: commit there, never merge to main)

Anh's report (two screen recordings, both in the repo): "the card flip flops on settle when scroll stops ... this happens on scroll UP". Evidence: `docs/reference/card-flipflop-up-recording.mp4` (2142x642, 30 fps, 6.5 s, Vertical mode, scrolling UP through Forbes / Similac / Sulwhasoo / music video; the clearer one) with a contact sheet `docs/reference/card-flipflop-up-sheet.png` (12 fps from t = 2.4 s), and the first recording `docs/reference/card-flipflop-settle-recording.mp4` + `card-flipflop-settle-sheet.png` (Sulwhasoo coming to rest, 15 fps from 1.0 s). Look at the frames before starting.

What is seen: when the strip decelerates and stops after a scroll UP, the image's warp does not ease to flat: it alternates between a strongly curved state and a flatter state, several times a second (Cowork measured the image's left edge at mid-height on the second recording, frames 120-148, about 0.9 s: it jumps between about 242-248 px (warped) and 200-215 px (flatter) in 3-5 frame groups, i.e. 6-10 Hz, and settles flat at about 191 px only about 1 s later). The first recording also shows the curvature changing side (convex top, then convex bottom) before it is flat. Scrolling DOWN does not show it.

### Hypothesis to test (Cowork read src/effects/cards-gl.js, do not assume)
The Work images' warp is a SIGNED spring (`bendI`, `bendI.amt = WARP_DIR * bendI.x * ...`, line ~537). Everything that differs between the two directions is chosen from the SIGN of `amt`: the camera and bow slots (`WP.camDown` 2.24 / `WP.camUp` 3.37, `bowDown` 0.085 / `bowUp` 0.315 swapped by WARP_DIR in the uShape / uBow uniforms), and the lean sign (`leanSignDown` -1 / `leanSignUp` +1, line ~439). If the spring (riseSec 2.2 / settleSec 1.9) or the speed curve (work/speed-curve.js, `speedKnee`) overshoots through zero, or the strip's velocity changes sign for a moment (the soft snap, snapDelay 1 s / snapSec 1, a small reverse pull, a trackpad tail), `amt` flips sign and the shader flips between the "up" and "down" parameter sets: with up and down this different, each flip reads as a flip-flop. On scroll down the two sets are closer in effect / the motion rarely reverses, so it is less visible.
