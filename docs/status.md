  SCROLL "PARTING" = live's lazyScrollAnimation chunk, reproduced EXACTLY (fit RMS 0.0px, 27 cards, 1920): per card each frame: d = cardCentreX - W/2, f = min(|d|/(W/2),1), sign = d<0?-1:1; g = 1 - clamp((cy - (-h)) / ((H+h) - (-h))) with cy = card centre in the viewport INCLUDING its own current translate; t = 1 - |g-0.5|*2 (triangle, 1 when cy ~ H/2); if g<=0 or g>=1 the card is NOT updated (keeps its last offset, hence the small leftovers). targetX = t*(0.01W + (0.2W - 0.01W)*(1-f))*sign*1.1; targetY = t*(0.01H + (0.3H - 0.01H)*(1-f))*1.1 (down); then offset += (target - offset)*0.2 PER FRAME. The 1.1 = 1 + 0.1*zIndex with zIndex = 1 (the prop is never passed). Starts only once 10% of the card has been visible (IntersectionObserver .1). 8 cards (ids 2,3,4,15,19,23,24,32, BackImageNoReact) have no scroll effect at all. It is NOT scroll-position-linked to the frame: it depends on the card's own screen position, so parting is strongest for cards nearest the centre line, and it is all proportional to W and H.

Applied to the card's parent; distance = card layer parallaxDistance (cards.json). Mouse stays 0 until the first mousemove.
