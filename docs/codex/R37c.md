# R37c: Work open / close, longer move + inverse-inflate rebound after landing

Planner: Cowork (Claude), 2026-10-11, from Anh's eye review of R37b. Builder: any agent. AGENTS.md applies (read it first). This brief
wins over docs/requests.md where they differ. Earlier briefs for context: docs/codex/R37-R38.md, docs/codex/R37b.md.

## Goal (what Anh should SEE)
Clicking a Work main image feels as dramatic as the reference: the card puffs and grows over a longer move, fills the screen, then the
picture inside rebounds the opposite way (bends inward), settles flat, and only then does the project page take over. Back = the reverse.

## Reference
- Video: docs/reference/r37-dribbble.mp4. Open at ~5.85 s; card fills the screen ~6.10 s; REBOUND ~6.10-6.50 s; slow zoom drift to ~6.80 s.
- Still sheet: docs/reference/r37c-reference-rebound.png (6.10, 6.16, 6.24 / 6.32, 6.44, 6.80 s, cropped centre). Look at the train
  window and the ribbed panel lines: after the card is full screen the CONTENT is bent inward (pincushion-like) and relaxes to straight,
  while it slowly zooms in.
- Extract your own frames 5.84-6.80 s every 0.02 s (container / VM ffmpeg, or node_modules/ffmpeg-static on Windows) into
  tools/scratch/r37c-ref/. Measure the rebound (line curvature or window-edge bow per frame) and the zoom (window width per frame).
- Do NOT copy: white page, curved text, stroke line, slider layout.
- Rough targets (starting values only, fit them):
  - workSec 0.4 -> 0.6. Reason (Anh): our image starts at ~55 % of the screen width, the reference card at ~25 %, so at the same time
    ours covers far less ground and feels like a nudge. The longer move compensates.
  - Rebound: one inward lobe, peak ~15 % of the bulge's peak strength, about 0.06 s after the card is full; damped settle to EXACTLY flat
    in reboundSec ~0.3 s. Zoom drift reboundZoom ~0.02-0.03 (content scale 1 -> 1 + z) over the rebound, then held so the hand-off
    matches (see step 3).

## Steps
1. Longer move: workSec start 0.6 (tunable). The bulge timing (bulgePeakAt / bulgeEndAt), the land point (workLandAt) and the push
   (workPushSec = landing time) are shares of the move, so they keep their shape and just play slower. Re-fit bulgeAmp / cornerAmp only
   if the longer move makes them look weaker or stronger than the reference.
2. Rebound (new phase, after the move): when the card is full screen, the GL quad keeps drawing for reboundSec more. In the FRAGMENT
   stage (texture coordinates), bend the content inward: radial lens on the box uv, uv' = c + (uv - c) * (1 + k * r^2) with k > 0 for an
   inward (pincushion) look; sign and form are to be confirmed against the reference frames: choose the one that matches. k follows a
   single damped lobe: 0 at the start of the phase, peak reboundAmp (relative to bulgeAmp) early, then a smooth settle to exactly 0 with
   zero slope at the end. The card OUTLINE stays the full screen rectangle (no mesh change, no background showing at the edges; clamp
   or extend sampling so edges never show a seam). Optional zoom drift: scale the sampled content toward the centre by reboundZoom over
   the phase.
3. Hand-off after the rebound: the last frame must be exactly what the project page header shows at scroll 0. If reboundZoom > 0 the
   last frame is zoomed, which the project page does NOT show: either ease the zoom back to 1 inside the phase, or (preferred if it
   looks right) keep the drift small and ease it back. The DOM hand-off overlap (bulgeHandoff) and sessionStorage `pxt` stay as they are.
   Navigation (location.href) happens after the rebound, so the total open is about workSec + reboundSec (~0.9 s). Start preloading the
   project page header as now; the extra time hides the load.
4. Way back (playBack): the rebound plays FIRST at full screen (reverse of the open: it bends inward, settles), then the bulge-and-shrink
   into the slot. Switch `backRebound` (1 on, 0 off) in case it feels slow on the way back. The wheel / key / pointer interruption must
   still jump cleanly to the end.
5. Layering check: in R37b's frame at 0.12 s (tools/scratch/r37b-shots/dark-open-6.png) the right neighbour (portrait) appears to be
   drawn ON TOP of the growing card's bulging edge. In the reference the opening card is always on top. Verify with a frame where the
   bulge passes over a neighbour; if the neighbour wins, put the transition canvas above the Work canvas / image layers (the project name
   and nav stay above, as now). Report what you found either way.
6. ?tune: add workSec (exists), reboundAmp, reboundSec, reboundZoom, backRebound to the TRANS list with descriptions in tune-docs.js;
   all in Copy values.

## Assumptions and likely misreadings
- A1. "Inverse inflate" = the CONTENT bends the opposite way after landing; it is not the card's outline shrinking or the size overshoot
  (the size overshoot is hidden by the screen edges and can stay as is).
- A2. The exact lens form and sign are the planner's guess. Match the reference frames, not the formula.
- A3. The slow zoom in the reference continues beyond 6.8 s (it may be the reference site's own header motion). Only include as much
  as reads well before the hand-off, and return it to 1 at the end (step 3).
- A4. Longer is Anh's call; 0.6 s is a start. Anh will tune by eye with ?tune.
- A5. The layering issue is a suspicion from one frame; verify before changing z-order.
- A6. R38 light theme: the rebound must look right on both themes (nothing of the page shows during it, since the card is full screen).
