## What to reuse, not rebuild
- The flight renderer and look: home-flight.js (`look: 'pixel'` = pointer-fx grid + synthetic brush). Lite caps: the R41 lite flight's maxDpr (transitionPolicy), smaller grid if frame time needs it (report).
- The hand-off records (localStorage, 10 s expiry, deleted on read), the return mark before leaving, `?debug=handoff`.
- The no-black-frame rule that finally worked on desktop: the cross-document view transition HOLDS the previous page's last flight frame (pagereveal + `html.r45-hold` keyframes, 1.5 s cap) until the new page's live strip / hero has drawn, then `skipTransition()`. iOS Safari supports cross-document view transitions from iOS 18.2: use them where present; where not, fall back to the first-paint layer (the R45 inline scripts) and measure the gap.
- Prerender (Speculation Rules) does nothing on Safari: do not rely on it on the phone.
- Apply the hold in BOTH directions on lite (Work -> Home too).
