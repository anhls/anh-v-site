# R42d: no wait before the open animation (cold click on a card starts moving at once)

Side: desktop (shared engine; phone/tablet effect stays off) · Folder: D:\Portfolio website\anh-v-site · Branch: desktop/r42d-open-lag (new, from work-v2 at 2b0ade8 or later)

Planner: Cowork, 2026-10-31. Builder: any agent (suggested Codex; AGENTS.md section 12: busy note first, one agent per folder). AGENTS.md applies (section 7: motion, section 9: check tiers). This brief wins over docs/requests.md.

## Goal (what Anh should SEE)
Click ANY project picture on the Work page, including one that just scrolled into the centre or one you never hovered: the picture starts its open animation (puff, inward bend, fill the screen) right away, with no visible pause and no progress cursor. Today a "cold" click can sit still for 0.5-2 s before it moves.

## What is known (Cowork read of the merged R42c code, 2026-10-31; confirm by measuring, do not assume)
R42c Part B (docs/codex/R42c-notes.md) made every click play the full bend, but a cold click WAITS for the full preparation before the flight clock starts. Measured then: worst click-to-flight wait 760 ms (60 Hz), 1207 ms (144 Hz), 2088 ms (4x CPU); a progress cursor covers the wait. The preparation path (src/effects/project-transition.js `prepareTransition` / `ensurePrepared`, src/effects/bulge-gl.js `createBulge` / `prepareTexture`) does, in order:
1. fetch + `decode()` the project's header image (`preloadHeader`), then decode it a second time as a fresh `Image` (`source.decode()`);
2. `createBulge`: a NEW WebGL context, canvas and shader program for every prepared renderer (disposed again on leave / swap), then waits frame by frame for `KHR_parallel_shader_compile`;
3. `prepareTexture`: uploads the texture in strips of `TRANSITION.hoverUploadRows` = 64 rows, ONE strip per animation frame, each strip through an async `createImageBitmap` resize. A 1440-row texture is about 23 frames at best; at 4x CPU or with the strip resize slower than a frame, far more. This strip pacing was made so HOVER preparation never hitches; on a CLICK it only adds delay.
Likely order of cost: (3) > (1, when the header is not cached) > (2). Measure before fixing.

## Assumptions and likely misreadings
