## What the report shows
Four backs were recorded. Two played the shrink (back-14573, back-41870: arrive -> shrink-end ~720 ms, frames <= 18 ms; one 60-64 ms frame before fly-start). The other two landed WITHOUT the shrink (back-6698, back-20009: marks only arrive -> idle-load-start at 381 / 449 ms) and that span is ONE frame of 415 / 484 ms: the page is frozen for half a second, which is the "after the swipe-back I cannot scroll for a moment" Anh feels. These are most likely the iOS edge swipes (R41f: when Safari already played its own slide, we land straight in the strip).
