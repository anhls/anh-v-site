## What Anh's iPhone showed after R41c (files in docs/reference/)
- `r41c-iphone-perf-report.json` (?perf report): the OPEN TRANSITION NOW RUNS on the iPhone: policy "lite (animated)", not weak (Apple GPU), guard step 0 / warp on, tap gate accepted, first flight frame 28 ms after the tap, 74 frames at 17 ms, no drops during the flight. The iPhone runs at 60 Hz (430x932 at DPR 3). NOTE: `longTasks` is always empty on iOS Safari (no Long Tasks API), so the report cannot show main-thread stalls there.
- `r41c-iphone-perf-2026-10-31.mp4` (re-encoded, original timing; recorded WITH the ?perf HUD drawing, which adds its own cost): stalls are smaller and fewer than in the first recording, but several remain: frozen frames mid-motion at 13.15 s (5 frames, ~83 ms), 15.62 s (5), 16.7 s (6, ~100 ms), 20.66 s (12, ~200 ms), 21.21 s (5).
- `r41c-iphone-stall-near-rest-sheet.png` (20.45-21.05 s, 30 fps): the pattern is consistent: the strip SLOWS DOWN near a project (counter 05/07), freezes ~200 ms while still coasting slowly, then jumps two projects (the next flick is applied late). The HUD's own graph shows a ~200 ms spike at that moment. So work that waits for "rest" fires while the strip is still coasting (slow but moving), and one of those jobs blocks the main thread on iOS.

## Steps
1. MEASURE on iOS-like conditions without the Long Tasks API: wrap every job that runs on "rest" or "near rest" (title focus / smear play and texture warm, soft snap start, idle prepare of the centred picture, R42d header prefetch / decode, counter / info updates, guard sampling, anything on a timer after a flick) with performance.now() and record the duration per job in the ?perf report (`restJobs: [{name, ms, stripSpeed, sinceTouchEnd}]`). Add `?perf=quiet` (records, no HUD drawing) so a test recording is not slowed by the HUD.
2. DEFINE REST ONCE: one shared "at rest" signal for all of these jobs: strip speed under a small threshold for at least ~150 ms AND no finger down AND no snap running. Nothing heavy runs before it. Any touch cancels pending rest work at once.
3. SLICE: every rest job must finish in under ~8 ms per frame on a 6x-throttled CPU, or be split across frames / idle callbacks; image decode only via `img.decode()` (async) or `createImageBitmap`, never a sync draw of an undecoded image; texture uploads one per frame at most.
4. Re-check that the soft snap never moves the strip against a new flick (the report's touch log shows snapCorrectionPx 48 px on one short touch at 21197 ms: check whether that is a snap pulling back).

## Targets
- 6x CPU, touch flicks at 390x844 and 430x932, with the strip coasting to a slow stop between flicks: no frame gap over ~33 ms (two 60 Hz frames) while the strip moves; every rest job measured, the slowest under ~8 ms per frame.
- The open transition keeps working (first frame <= 100 ms, 0 drops), back too.
- On Anh's iPhone: a new recording with `?perf=quiet` shows no freeze longer than ~2 frames (Cowork measures it the same way).
