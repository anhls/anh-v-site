# R41h notes (Claude, 2026-11-01, branch lite/r41e)

1. **Any visible picture opens.** The centre rule is gone from the lite tap gate (`mode-lite.js` `intent`; `centre` is still logged). The flight already starts from the tapped picture's own rect (bx/by of that item); `resume()` re-centres the strip on the project at the back, so back lands centred. Test (Chromium touch, 390x844): 12 of 12 taps on visible non-centred pictures (above and below the centre) opened THAT project, and after back it was centred (< 5 % of the height off). Old unit expectations about `centreTol` in tools/scratch/r41/gate.test.mjs no longer apply (spec change, not a weakened check).
2. **Taps while the strip still moves.** One definition (`stripMoving`): a transition running, or strip speed over **TAP_FLING = 300 px/s**. Touch at or under it stops the snap / coast at once and, if it ends as a tap, opens; over it the touch only stops the strip. Test: 8 touches during a real fling (2300-3500 px/s) -> 0 opens, the strip stops; taps while it coasted at 20-280 px/s -> 8 of 8 opened (one earlier run showed 6 of 8: one refused at 316 px/s = over the limit, one had no click at all: Chromium eats the tap that stops a coasting strip). Added a fallback: a tap (up, no drag) on a picture with no click within 90 ms is clicked by us; the gate and lock still decide, no double open seen.
3. **Input locked after the back.** Found: the back overlay `#pxb` (position:fixed, full screen, z-index 8) took the touches for the whole shrink (~0.85 s) and iOS then treats the touch as a page gesture. It now has `pointer-events:none` (the capture listeners on window still see the touch, which still finishes the shrink at once). Test (Chromium, drag 50 ms after the strip is visible again, 5 swipe-backs + 5 browser Backs): the drag moved 240 px every time (before the change: 4 of 8 runs moved only 40 px after the first few). NOT reproduced as a long lock in Chromium; the phone decides. If a lock remains: the `tapLog` / `titlePlays` of that moment, plus the in-place back is now a run in the report.
4. **backRuns.** The in-place back (swipe and browser Back) starts a run with `back:arrive` in `page-swap.js back()`; the report keeps 24 runs (was 8). Test: 5 + 5 backs -> 8 saved at the old cap; cap raised, so 20 fit.
