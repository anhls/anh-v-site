## Fix 1: no loader when coming back from Work
- The loader is for a fresh first visit only. Arriving from Work by ANY path (popstate Back after the flight, the wordmark, the direct-visit `pxg` path, a reload of the homepage document instead of a bfcache restore) skips the loader and paints the hero at its rest state from the first frame (the R45 `pxg` inline-script path), so the growing card lands on the hero, not on the loader.
- Mark the return in sessionStorage BEFORE leaving Work (history.back / go(-2) / navigation) so the homepage knows on its first paint, whatever the browser does with its cache. Clear the mark after use. A normal fresh visit (new tab, typed URL) still shows the loader.
- Test: home -> Work -> Back, home -> Work -> wordmark, direct /work/ -> wordmark, each 5 times; also with the cache disabled (DevTools "Disable cache", which forces a real reload): the loader never shows on these paths.

## Fix 2: land on the full strip, not a lone card on black
Goal: the last frame of the flight IS the Work page at rest: the first card in its place AND its neighbours in theirs, on the page colour, so the hand-off cannot be seen.
- Home side (home-flight.js): in the second half of the flight (start ~0.45 of progress, tunable `neighboursFrom`), the neighbour cards that are visible at the Work page's start offset (current mode Side or Vertical, the same files and crops and rest rectangles the strip uses; compute them from the same layout code / WORK_V2 numbers, not by hand) slide in from outside the screen along the strip axis with the same smear, easing into their exact rest rectangles at progress 1. The background goes from the hero to the Work page colour (not black-then-colour).
- Work side (inline head script + home-return.js): the `#pxh` first-paint layer paints the SAME full set (first card + neighbours, same rects), not only the first card, and is removed when the live strip has drawn those cards (not on a fixed timer). The Work text UI (title, counter, labels, rulers) plays its own entrance timed with the cards settling, never pops in after a black gap.
- Neighbour textures load on idle with the rest of the warm-up (respect Save-Data; if a neighbour is not ready at the click, it may join later in the flight, never block the click).
- Landing check: the final flight frame vs a direct /work/ screenshot at rest (cards area): invisible difference only (AGENTS.md 3), at 1920 and 2560, both modes.

## Fix 3: a smear that reads
Today the trail is `smearStrength` (0.08) of the way travelled, made of scaled copies of the picture: in a shrink this becomes a faint zoom ghost, which reads as a fade. Anh wants the Work page's smear language.
- Make the smear DIRECTIONAL and visible: streaky displacement along the motion (the pointer-fx pixel-grid look: blocky / per-row offsets with noise, plus the small RGB split), strongest at peak speed, exactly 0 at rest. Reuse the shader ideas from src/work/pointer-fx.js and the title corner-pull smear; do not invent a new look.
- Strength: start much higher (e.g. peak displacement ~6-10 % of the picture width, smearRGB ~0.01) and tune until a mid-flight frame shows the smear at a glance; then Anh fine-tunes in ?tune. The cross-fade must not hide it (the smear runs on both pictures during the cross-fade).
- Time-based only: the same visible smear at 60 and 144 Hz (judge from progress / elapsed time, never from the previous frame).
- Same on the way back (card -> hero).
- "Looks right?" (AGENTS.md 2): contact sheet of the flight at 6 progress points (0.1 .. 0.9) next to a Work page pointer-smear frame. Line 1 must be "yes: smear clearly visible" before calling it done.
