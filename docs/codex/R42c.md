## Goal (what Anh should SEE)
Open: the card puffs out as it starts to grow, then CLEARLY flips to an inward bend (edges dip in, corners reach out) as it fills
the screen, then settles flat. Back: a gentle inward bend with nearly sharp corners, a fast drop and then a soft ~0.5 s settle onto
the card. Both read like docs/reference/r37-dribbble.mp4 at a glance.

## What Cowork measured (frames from the reference and Anh's recording 2026-10-11 23:17; sheet r42b-back-vs-dribbble.jpg)
- Reference OPEN (r37-dribbble.mp4 about 5.6 to 6.2 s): 5.65 to 5.75 s small outward puff; about 5.85 to 6.1 s, while the card grows
  to full screen, the edges bow INWARD (top / bottom edge midpoints sit lower / higher than the corners, corners flare out); flat at rest.
  Anh: "the opening animation lacks that inward inflate, or the strength is too low to see". Ours: the inward phase is not visible
  (TRANSITION.reboundAmp 0.1 in src/settings/desktop.js is the likely knob, but confirm in bulge-gl.js).
- Reference BACK (about 7.8 to 8.4 s): drops from full screen to near card size in about 0.25 s with a SMALL inward bend and nearly
  square corners, then keeps shrinking slowly for about another 0.4 to 0.5 s.
- Ours BACK (after R42b): a STRONG OUTWARD pillow with big rounded corners (the open shape reused), about 0.33 s, then stops.
  So the direction is wrong, the strength is far too high and the tail is too short.

## Method (measure, then fit: CLAUDE.md, AGENTS.md section 7)
1. Extract reference frames at full rate for both phases. For each frame measure the edge bow: the distance of the top, bottom,
   left and right edge midpoints from the straight line between their corners, as % of the card's height / width (+ = outward,
   - = inward), plus the corner radius and the card size. Same for ours (Playwright, rAF paced).
2. Plot both curves over normalised time (0 = trigger, 1 = settle). Fit our parameters to the reference curve SHAPES:
   sign, peak value, peak time, and how long each phase lasts. Start values are the measured ones, not guesses.
3. Back gets its own settings if the open ones cannot express it (e.g. backBendAmp negative, backCornerAmp small, backSec / tail).
   Put every new number in TRANSITION in src/settings/desktop.js with a comment, and in the ?tune panel if the panel already has a
   transition group.
