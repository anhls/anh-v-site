# R41g notes (Claude, 2026-11-01, branch lite/r41e)

## Part A: taps that replayed the title
- **Cause (found by reading the code; the phone's own refusing term was not logged before):** the title counts the strip as "stopped" once its speed is under `scrollStopSpeed x view length` (0.05 x 844 = about 42 px/s) and plays, while the tap gate called the strip "moving" at any speed over 5 px/s, or while the inertia / snap OBJECT still existed. The strip needs a while to decay from 42 to 5 px/s, so a tap right after the title appeared was refused as a grab; the touchdown also reset rest and hid the title, and it replayed 250-300 ms later (what Anh saw).
- **Fix:** one definition, `stripMoving()` in core.js = a transition running, or speed over max(5, the title's stopped speed). Used for the touchdown snapshot, the click verdict and the next point. A finger put down on a strip that is at rest no longer calls `rest.interrupt()` / sets `inputAt` / `wmScrolled`: that runs at the first move past the drag threshold (`drag.begin`), so a plain tap neither hides the title nor resets rest.
- **Logging:** `?perf` report field `tapLog`: every touchdown (busy, inertia, snap, speed, restFor) and every click (accepted, or `refused` = dragMoved / moving(now) / movingAtDown / longPress / locked / off-centre / paused / transitionBusy, plus all gate terms). `titlePlays` logs the strip-moving cancel once per move (was every frame).
- **Tests (Chromium touch emulation, 100 ms press):** 20 of 20 taps open at 390x844 6x CPU and 430x932 1x; 0 title hides before the open. A tap while the strip still drifts at 38 px/s (between the old and new limits) opens. NOTE: the baseline also passed my 20-tap test (Chromium's strip settles faster than the phone's), so the phone is the proof; the tapLog will show it if a tap is still refused.

## Part B: swipe right = back (option b)
- In page-swap.js (touch listeners, only while a project page is open): a one-finger touch that starts more than 24 px from the left edge (the edge swipe stays Safari's, R41f), is horizontal if dx > 2x |dy| within the first 12 px, and is not inside a horizontally scrollable element. Then the page follows the finger 35 % (max 36 px; chosen, optional per the brief); release past 25 % of the width, or a quick flick (> 0.5 px/ms and > 60 px) -> `back(true)` = the Back button path (history.back -> popstate -> glide if scrolled + shrink); otherwise it springs back in 0.18 s. A vertical or undecided touch is never touched (listener is passive until it is horizontal).
- Tests: 20 of 20 swipes -> 20 shrinks (marks back:shrink-end each) at 390x844; 6 of 6 at 430x932 and at 820x1180 (tablet); 5 vertical scrolls (the page scrolled) and 3 short slow horizontal moves -> 0 backs. The glide for a scrolled page is the existing one (cap 0.3 s).
- Anh checks by hand: the follow feel (35 %), the 25 % / flick limits, and that nothing on a project page (galleries, links) fights the swipe.
